# Progress (evidence so far; VERIFIED.md is written when every criterion has its line)

Slices 1 to 2b-1 are built (see the sections below); slice 1 (the registry and run claims, `oss_format::alert_registry`, `oss_meta::AlertRegistry`) is built; the monitor store (its documents and the per-tenant monitor index the runner and the removal's question read) comes with slice 2, where the routes need it. Nothing else is built.

- AC-2 (registry part): `registering_costs_one_read_and_one_write_even_when_the_entry_is_there`, `a_removal_costs_one_read_and_one_write` (emulator and S3 on loopback: 1 GET and 1 PUT; the file store adds one ranged read to a swap), rows `alerting.register.requests` and `alerting.unregister.requests` in [request-budgets](../../request-budgets.md). The monitor's own put, read and delete counts are slice 2's.
- Registry protocol (slice 5's "Registry writes"): `a_registration_between_the_removals_read_and_swap_keeps_the_entry`, `a_registration_after_the_removal_adds_the_entry_back`, `a_removal_that_stops_before_its_swap_leaves_the_entry`, `a_removal_that_finds_an_enabled_monitor_keeps_the_entry_and_writes_nothing`, `a_removal_that_keeps_losing_its_swap_gives_up_to_contention`, over the three backends. Seen red against two broken protocols: a registration that skips its swap when the entry is present (3 of the tests failed) and a removal that asks its question only for a never-created shard (4 failed). A remover killed between any two steps is the stopped-before-the-swap test: nothing is written before the swap.
- Run claim (slice 5's "Claim"): `a_slot_is_claimed_once_and_only_upwards` (row `alerting.claim.requests`: a first claim is one read and one create), `a_hinted_claim_is_one_request_and_a_stale_hint_falls_back_to_a_read` (row `alerting.claim_hinted.requests`: with the generation of the node's last claim, one swap), `of_two_claimers_of_one_slot_exactly_one_wins` and `a_claim_that_keeps_losing_its_swap_gives_up_to_contention` (the register moved between a read and the swap, through `claim_between`), `a_damaged_claim_register_is_refused`. Claim registers are never deleted here; removing a deleted monitor's claim is slice 2's.
- Formats: `crates/format/tests/alert_registry.rs` (shard order, bounds, damaged shards refused, tenant id rules, shard hash `registry_shard_of("a") == 0x2c` from FNV-1a, keys, claim bytes). Seen red against a stub of every function (9 of 9 failed).
- Bound: a shard past 4,096 tenants refuses a new tenant (`a_full_shard_refuses_a_new_tenant_and_keeps_a_present_one`); a tenant already named still registers.

Not run: the spec's concurrent soak across nodes (slice 5); the registry's bytes read per TTL at 1 million tenants.

## Slice 2a: the monitor index and the store

- `crates/format/tests/monitor_index.rs`: the index format (id order, bounds, damaged indexes refused, keys). Every test of the format was written with the code and then held by mutation: the first run of `cargo mutants` on `monitor_index.rs` missed five mutants (a size bound, three duplicate-field guards, an edge), each now has a test, and all 65 are caught or unviable.
- `crates/meta/tests/descriptors/monitors.rs` (emulator, S3 on loopback, file store): put, read, replace, delete, `require_absent`, no blob left behind, the tenant registered by an enabled put and removed by the delete or disable of its last enabled monitor and not before, tenants kept apart, bounds. Row `alerting.monitor_put.requests`. All 41 mutants of `monitors.rs` are caught or unviable (three were missed first and got tests: the index's content, the body bound's edge, and that a disabled put beside an enabled monitor makes no registry request).
- AC-2 as built: a put is the index read, the body's write and the index's swap (1 GET and 2 PUTs), an enabled put adds the registry's shard read and swap (1 GET and 1 PUT), a delete or disable of the last enabled monitor adds the shard read, the index read and the shard swap. The spec's "before and after" shard reads are superseded by the always-swap protocol of slice 5.
- Not here: the routes (slice 2b), `_search`, `_execute`, alerts.

## Slice 2b-1: monitor create, replace, read and delete

- `crates/node/tests/server/alerting_routes.rs`, through HTTP on a store: `POST|PUT|GET|DELETE /_plugins/_alerting/monitors[/{id}]` and the same on `/_opendistro/_alerting`; a body that fails a check is 400 and stores nothing (missing name, unknown `monitor_type`, a period of 0 or a unit outside MINUTES, HOURS and DAYS, no inputs, 101 triggers, 101 actions, malformed JSON); the schedule floor (1 minute allowed, refused one second over a floor of 61); tenants kept apart by the `securitytenant` header (`_default_` without one; a tenant with a `/` is 400), an enabled monitor registering its tenant and a delete of its last one removing it. The monitor routes' answers and refusals are replayed against the stock 3.10.0 recording (`alerting_stock.rs`, steps 0 to 28 and 55 to 68, no listed gaps): a monitor's `_version` and `_seq_no` are kept per monitor in the index entry (`v`, `q`), `if_seq_no` conflicts are 409, an unknown input index is 404, an unknown `monitor_type` is 500 as the plugin answers. `POST|GET /monitors/_search` answers from the index and the bodies with no search engine: `match_all`, `match_none`, `ids`, `term`, `terms`, `match`, `match_phrase`, `prefix`, `wildcard`, `range`, `exists` and `bool`, a field sort, `from` and `size` (window 10,000), ties in the order the index wrote them; a `match_all` with no sort reads only the page's bodies, any other search reads every body (rows `alerting.monitor_search_page.requests` and `alerting.monitor_search_filtered.requests`). Other clauses (`query_string` and the like) are 400. The other routes (execute, destinations, alerts, stats) are not replayed yet.
- The routes are on without a setting, and so will the runner be: plugins have no enable switch (owner's rule, 2026-11-28; spec "Switches"). The minimum interval is `AlertingService::with_min_interval`, default 60 s; its setting is slice 2b-2's.
- `_version` and `_seq_no` are the tenant's index change count, not one per monitor; `if_seq_no` and `if_primary_term` are accepted and not yet checked. Cron schedules are stored and not yet validated or floor-checked.
- Not here: `_search`, `_execute`, alerts, destinations, `_stats`, notification channel configs.
