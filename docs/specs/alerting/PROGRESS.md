## Slice 5: the runner

`crates/node/src/alerting_run/schedule.rs` (`Scheduler`), ticked every 5 s by `boot/alerting_loop.rs` on every node, with no setting (plugins have none). A refresh (at most once a minute, or when the live nodes change) reads the 256 registry shards, each registered tenant's monitor index, and the bodies of the enabled period monitors this node tries that it does not hold; between refreshes a tick is arithmetic over the cache and sends no request (asserted by the request meter in `alerting_run.rs`). A monitor is tried by the two live nodes with the highest rendezvous weight for `tenant/monitor`; a slot is `latest_due` (the AD slot arithmetic, `ad_route::slots`, here with `max_skew` 5 s and `visibility_lag` 5 s), claimed by `ScheduleRegistry::claim` with the generation hint of this node's last claim, and run only by the claim's winner through `AlertingService::run_scheduled`, which records the alerts as an execute without `dryrun` does. Three nodes over one monitor run each slot once and no more than two of them try it (test `a_monitor_runs_once_a_slot_across_three_nodes_and_an_idle_tick_is_free`). Not done: cron schedules (no run), a bucket- or document-level monitor (501 until slice 4), history and actions.
