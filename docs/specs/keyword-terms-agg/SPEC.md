# Reader: `terms` aggregation over a keyword field

## Serves

Roadmap R10.2, after the numeric [terms-agg](../terms-agg/SPEC.md): OpenSearch's `terms` on a keyword field of a single-shard `_search`, with a shard-level result a coordinator reduces across shards (I-13, REST matches OpenSearch; ADR 0004, bounded memory).

## Change

`oss-reader` serves `terms` over a keyword field with the same keys as the numeric one: `field`, `size`, `shard_size`, `min_doc_count` (at least 1) and `order` (`_count` or `_key`, one criterion). The shard counts with lucene-aggs' keyword `terms` (the port of `GlobalOrdinalsStringTermsAggregator`) over the field's global ordinals, keeps what `StringTerms` holds (`aggs::bucket::keyword_terms::KeywordTermsResult`: keys as text), reduces shard results as `InternalTerms.reduce` (reached through `partial::reduce`, so `oss-coordinator`'s `reduce_shards` serves it) and writes it as `StringTerms.doXContentBody` does. The parser and `TermsSpec` are the numeric aggregation's; the shard picks the result by the field's mapped type.

Global ordinals are built per search from the segments' term dictionaries. Their memory is reserved with the hits before the search starts, from sizes the segments' metadata states (`terms_dict_size`, `max_term_length`), so nothing is sized by reading the dictionaries first: a field whose dictionaries do not fit the pool refuses the search, rather than growing without bound.

Second slice: `min_doc_count: 0` (every accepted term of the shard is a candidate bucket, counted or not, as `collectionStrategy.forEach` lists them; the shard keeps `shard_size`), `include` and `exclude` (a Lucene regular expression the whole term must match, or an array of terms; both of one kind, include first and exclude after it, as `IncludeExclude.merge` requires) and `missing` (a string: the term a document without a value counts under, numbered among the ordinals where it sorts when no segment holds it). Their parse errors are OpenSearch's; a pattern that does not parse or is over 1,000 characters fails the shard as OpenSearch fails it, and one too large to determinize is refused as not supported yet. On a numeric field `include`, `exclude` and `missing` are refused as not supported yet.

Not in this change, each refused with "not supported by this node yet": a partition-based `include`, a `missing` that is not a string, `script`, `value_type`, `format`, `execution_hint`, `collect_mode`, `show_term_doc_count_error`, `shard_min_doc_count`, sub-aggregations other than those of the third slice, and `order` by a sub-aggregation or with several criteria. A text field still fails as OpenSearch fails it.

Third slice: sub-aggregations of a keyword `terms`, the single-value and stats metrics (`min`, `max`, `sum`, `avg`, `stats`, `value_count`) over a numeric or date field. The engine collects each into the bucket of the document's term; the shard result carries one value a kept bucket and sub-aggregation; the reduce merges the values of equal keys with the metric's own reduce; each bucket writes its sub-aggregations after `doc_count` in the order `Aggregations.toXContent` lists them. A sub-aggregation of another kind, and any sub-aggregation of a numeric `terms`, is refused as not supported yet (a `histogram`'s still is); a sub-aggregation of a metric is OpenSearch's `aggregation_initialization_exception`.

Fourth slice: a keyword `terms` as a sub-aggregation of a keyword `terms`, to any depth, each with its own scalar or `terms` sub-aggregations. The engine counts the inner terms per outer bucket (ordinals of the inner field are built once a field); each outer bucket carries the inner `terms` result as its sub-aggregation value, which reduces and writes as any keyword `terms`. A numeric `terms`, a `histogram`, and every other kind remain refused as not supported yet.

Fifth slice: `order` of a keyword `terms` by a metric sub-aggregation: `{"m": "desc"}`, `{"m.value": "asc"}`, `{"st.max": "asc"}` or `{"m": ...}` with `m[key]`, one criterion, the term ascending breaking ties (`BucketOrder.compound`'s tie-breaker). A shard cannot know the best buckets before it has counted them all, so it keeps every term's bucket (at most the bucket limit, as for `min_doc_count: 0`) and cuts to `shard_size` after ordering, the way `BucketSelectionStrategy` does; a metric with no value (an `avg` of no document) is NaN and sorts last whichever the direction (`Comparators.compareDiscardNaN`). The path is checked as `AggregationPath` and the aggregators check it, even for an unmapped field. A numeric `terms` ordered by a sub-aggregation, a path through several aggregations, and an order of several criteria remain refused as not supported yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1: on an index of several segments, the buckets of a keyword field are its distinct terms with their document counts over the query's matches, ordered by count descending then term ascending, cut at `size`, with `sum_other_doc_count` the documents of the buckets cut; `order` by `_key` and by `_count` ascending list as OpenSearch lists them; keys compare as bytes.
- AC-2: `min_doc_count` above 1 is applied by the reduce and counts the dropped buckets' documents in `sum_other_doc_count`; an unmapped field and a match-none query give no buckets; every key listed as not in this change is refused as not supported yet.
- AC-3: shard results reduce as `InternalTerms.reduce` does for the numeric one (equal keys add, `min_doc_count` filters, the order sorts, `size` cuts into `sum_other_doc_count`, `doc_count_error_upper_bound` is 0 for one shard and otherwise the sum of what each full shard may have cut); results of different kinds (a keyword and a numeric `terms` of one name) do not reduce.
- AC-4: the response writes `doc_count_error_upper_bound`, `sum_other_doc_count` and `buckets`, each bucket `key` (a JSON string, escaped as OpenSearch escapes it) then `doc_count`.
- AC-5: the search reserves a keyword `terms`' memory with its hits: for each segment holding the field, 40 bytes a term of its dictionary (the ordinal maps and the counts), twice its longest term (the merge's term buffers), and for each of `shard_size` buckets (at most the bucket limit and the terms there are) three copies of the longest term of any segment and 64 bytes of headers and counts (the engine's key, the result's and a reduce's); a field whose mapping is not a keyword is reserved as before.
- AC-6: the cold and warm requests of a term search with a keyword `terms` beside its metrics are held to the rows `search.keyword_terms.*` of [request-budgets.md](../../request-budgets.md).
- AC-7: `include` and `exclude` keep the accepted terms' buckets only (regular expressions match the whole term, sets list terms, exclude runs after include, a rejected term's documents are not `sum_other_doc_count`); `min_doc_count: 0` lists the accepted terms no match holds as empty buckets, cut at `shard_size`, ordered like the others; a `missing` term no segment holds is a bucket among the ordinals (a filter numbers it where it sorts) and one a segment holds adds none; a pattern that does not parse, or is over 1,000 UTF-16 units, fails the shard with OpenSearch's message; a numeric field refuses the three.
- AC-8: the reservation adds 32 bytes a dictionary term under `min_doc_count: 0` and, under a filter, two copies of the accepted bits (a bit a term and a word) and the longest term.
- AC-9: `include`, `exclude` and `missing` parse as `TermsAggregationBuilder`'s parser parses them: a string or an array of strings or values for `include` and `exclude` (anything else is "Unrecognized token for an include [...]"), arrays sorted without repeats, a regular expression and a set not mixed ("Cannot mix a regex-based include with a set-based method", naming as the include the method of the later of the two when that is `include`, else of the earlier), a partition-based `include` and a `missing` that is not a string refused as not supported yet.
- AC-10: each bucket of a keyword `terms` with `min`, `max`, `sum`, `avg`, `stats` or `value_count` sub-aggregations holds those over the documents of its term, under the query, a date field written with its `value_as_string`; a bucket whose documents have no value writes the empty metric (`null`, a `sum` of `0.0`); a sub-aggregation of an unmapped field is the empty metric in every bucket; a keyword field refuses it as OpenSearch refuses it.
- AC-11: shard results reduce the sub-aggregations of equal keys with the metric's reduce (an `avg` adds sums and counts, a `min` takes the smallest), a bucket on one shard only keeps its values, and the buckets cut by `size` take theirs with them.
- AC-12: a bucket writes its sub-aggregations after `doc_count`, in the order `Aggregations.toXContent` lists sibling aggregations.
- AC-13: the search reserves a keyword `terms`' sub-aggregations with it: for each term of the dictionaries a metric state a sub-aggregation (the engine's), and for each kept bucket a result value a sub-aggregation; a sub-aggregation of another kind is refused as not supported yet at parse and one under a numeric `terms` at the shard, `histogram`'s refusal unchanged.
- AC-14: a keyword `terms` under a keyword `terms` counts, in each outer bucket, the inner terms of that bucket's documents under the query (with the inner `size`, `order`, `min_doc_count`, `include`, `exclude` and `missing`, cut with its own `sum_other_doc_count`), and may hold scalar sub-aggregations and further `terms`; an inner `terms` of an unmapped field has no buckets in every outer bucket, and one of a numeric field is refused as not supported yet.
- AC-15: nested results reduce across shards (an inner `terms` of equal outer keys reduces as a keyword `terms` does, `doc_count_error_upper_bound` included) and write inside their outer bucket after `doc_count`, in the order of the sub-aggregations' names.
- AC-16: the search reserves an inner `terms` as a keyword `terms` whose kept buckets are `shard_size` (at most its terms) of each outer bucket kept, at most the bucket limit, plus its own ordinals and, for every (outer cell, term) pair, at most the bucket limit, its hash entry, its sub-aggregation states and a candidate bucket under `min_doc_count: 0`, where an outer cell is a term the outer `terms` counts (kept or not).
- AC-17: a keyword `terms` ordered by a metric sub-aggregation lists buckets by that metric (direction as given, NaN last, equal metrics by term ascending); the shard keeps the best `shard_size` by it with the cut buckets' documents in `sum_other_doc_count`, the reduce orders the reduced buckets by the reduced metric and cuts at `size`, and `doc_count_error_upper_bound` is -1 when a shard returned `shard_size` buckets and 0 when none did; it holds for a nested `terms` ordered within each outer bucket.
- AC-18: the order's path is checked as OpenSearch checks it, and a bad one fails the shard with `aggregation_execution_exception` ("Invalid aggregation order path [p]. ..."): a sub-aggregation that does not exist, a key on a single-value metric other than `value`, no key or an unknown one on `stats`, a `terms` as the target, a path through another aggregation, a key before the last element, and a malformed element ("Invalid path element [e] in path [p]", at parse); it is checked for an unmapped field too; a numeric `terms` refuses an order by a sub-aggregation as not supported yet.
- AC-19: the reservation counts every dictionary term as a kept bucket (at most the bucket limit) for a `terms` ordered by a sub-aggregation, where `shard_size` bounds it otherwise.

## Test plan

Unit tests in `aggs/bucket/keyword_terms_tests.rs` (reduce, writing: AC-3, AC-4), `aggs/bucket/keyword_collect_tests.rs` over an `oss-testkit` index of four segments (AC-1, AC-2, AC-5), `crates/coordinator/tests/keyword_terms_reduce.rs` (AC-3 across shards), the budget gate in `crates/node/tests/it/aggs_budgets.rs` (AC-6); the second slice in `keyword_options_tests.rs` (AC-9) and the end of `keyword_collect_tests.rs` (AC-7, AC-8); the third in the same files (AC-10 to AC-13; AC-14 to AC-16 in `keyword_subs_tests.rs`) and `crates/coordinator/tests/keyword_terms_reduce.rs` (AC-11).

## S3 request budget

Measured, not assumed: AC-6 holds the cold request and round-trip counts of a keyword `terms` beside the metric aggregations. LIST 0, HEAD 0, PUT 0; an idle index makes none. The same row holds with `max` sub-aggregations of the keyword `terms`: they add no request, whatever their number. A keyword `terms` of a second field under one is held equal to the plain `terms`' cold request count (`a_nested_keyword_terms_adds_no_request_to_a_cold_search`, 15 GETs on the built index both ways).

## Switch

None: a keyword `terms` was refused before, and a request without one is unchanged.

## Risks

No stock 3.10.0 recording backs these steps, so the expected bodies are read from the Java. The reservation is read from segment metadata: an upper bound on the terms, not a measurement of the engine's allocations. A multi-shard search reserves the hits of shard 0 for every shard (`crates/node/src/coordinate.rs`), so shards with larger dictionaries than shard 0 are under-reserved; that path belongs to the node assembly. A keyword `terms` of an index mapped as a keyword in one place and unmapped in another does not reduce with the unmapped shard's empty numeric result. `include` and `exclude` read every dictionary term once per search; a pattern too large to determinize is refused as not supported yet, where OpenSearch fails it with `TooComplexToDeterminizeException`, which is CPU a stock node spends too. Global ordinals are rebuilt on every search of an open reader that has not kept them; a reader that keeps them holds their bytes beyond the request.
